Flight Bookings

Every great journey has a boarding pass in its origin story. We watch fare windows, flag the hidden stopovers, and know which seats are genuinely worth the upgrade. Whether it's a red-eye to Dubai or a Cessna to a bush strip, you board with total clarity.

Hotel Reservations

We match the property to your pace, your pillow standards and your pocket, then secure the rates, the room category and the little extras that turn a stay into a story.

  • Rate negotiation & loyalty-program stacking

  • Special-occasion setups: anniversaries, honeymoons, surprises

First Step Abroad

Passport Applications

No passport, no poetry. Kenya's passport process rewards preparation and punishes guesswork — our documents desk has walked hundreds of travellers through first applications, renewals, children's passports and the replacement queues. We check every photo against the official specification, every field against current guidelines, and book the earliest appointment slots — so the only thing left for you to do is show up and smile at the camera.

  • Document checklist tailored to your exact case
  • Photo & bio-form pre-checks before you pay a shilling
  • Appointment booking at the earliest available slot

Borders, Opened

Visa Applications

Visa rules change like weather — our job is to be the forecast. We prepare tourist, business and transit applications for the UK, USA, Schengen states, UAE, South Africa, Asia and beyond: the right form, the right letter, the right order of pages. Every dossier is reviewed document-by-document before anything is submitted, and we track each application through to the stamp — including the awkward follow-up calls nobody wants to make.

  • Country-specific requirement briefs that reflect this week's rules
  • Full dossier assembly & document review
  • Appointment & biometrics scheduling

07

Fast-Track Entry

Kenya eTA Applications

Kenya's electronic Travel Authorisation replaced the old visa-on-arrival for most visitors — and the portal rewards precision: one mismatched detail and the application stalls. We've submitted on behalf of families, conference delegations and honeymooners from every continent, getting details right the first time, chasing approvals and handling corrections without drama. Arrive with the authorisation already in your inbox and a warm breeze as the only welcome queue.

  • Correct-first-time eTA preparation
  • Family & group applications batched together
  • Approval chasing & proactive status updates

08

Peace of Mind

Travel Insurance

The one purchase you hope to never use — and the one you should never travel without. We place medical, cancellation and baggage cover from trusted, claims-proven underwriters, sized to the trip: a weekend in Zanzibar needs different cover than a Kilimanjaro push. Policies are explained in plain language — what's covered, what isn't, and exactly how a claim works — because a policy you don't understand isn't protection; it's paper.

  • Trip-sized cover from vetted underwriters
  • Medical, cancellation & baggage protection
  • Adventure & high-altitude activity riders
