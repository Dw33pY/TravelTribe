Our Story

Born in Nairobi. Fluent everywhere.

We didn't set out to build a travel company. We set out to build the tribe we wished existed when we were the ones standing at the booking counter with a hundred questions.

How the tribe came to be

Travel Tribe Vacations began the way most good journeys do — with a group of friends arguing over who makes the better itinerary. We had spent years criss-crossing Kenya and the region for work, for weddings and for the sheer joy of a long Rift Valley road, and somewhere along the way people started asking us to plan their trips too. What began as a favour became a vocation; a vocation became a company.

The name is deliberate. A tribe, in the oldest sense of the word, is a group that carries its members — that knows where the water is in a dry season, that sends someone ahead to prepare the next camp, that you can call at midnight without apology. That is what we built: a company structured less like a counter and more like a family of specialists, each fluent in one dialect of travel.

"We Speak Travel" is our promise and our grammar. Flights, hotels, visas, eTA and insurance are the vocabulary — necessary, but useless without fluency. Fluency is knowing which camp has the best seats for a river crossing, which embassy is moving slowly this quarter, which flight connection is a gamble disguised as a bargain. We speak it so you don't have to learn it.

Today we design journeys across Kenya, East Africa and further afield — Egypt, the Gulf, Asia, Europe — for travellers who want the wild without the wild guesswork. The company has grown; the argument about who makes the better itinerary has not been resolved. Frankly, it's our quality-control system.

  • The three promises we never break
  • Every itinerary is bespoke — we have never sent two identical journeys out the door.
  • Every price is transparent — the quote you approve is the invoice you pay.
  • Every client is covered — a real human from the tribe is reachable, all journey long.

Mission and vision

Our mission

To make world-class travel effortless for every client — handling every document, flight, bed and dawn between the dream and the departure gate — so the traveller is free to actually travel.

Our vision

To be East Africa's most trusted travel tribe: the first name remembered when a Kenyan imagines a journey, and the first name recommended when a visitor lands in one.

How We're Built

Why Kenya First

The whole world, in one country's borders

There is a reason safaris were invented here. Kenya compresses a continent into a border: glaciers on the equator, rainforest to desert within a morning's drive, the world's greatest wildlife migration and a coastline that has been trading with the world for a thousand years. Few countries hand a traveller this much, this close together.

Being born here is our unfair advantage — we know which ranger station opens early, which airstrip floods in April, and which community conservancy is quietly outperforming the big reserves this season. We spend that local fluency on every client, whether their journey starts in Nairobi or ends in it.

Born here. Guiding here. Answerable here.
