The Signature

Safaris, day by golden day

Four signature journeys through Kenya's wild heart — each shown with its full day-by-day itinerary. These are starting points: your safari is tailored around you until it feels inevitable.

A note on pricing: the figures below are honest starting-from guides (USD, per person, sharing). Season, camp tier and party size move the number — your consultant confirms an exact, all-in quote before anything is booked.

The journey, day by day

  1. 1

    Day 1

    Nairobi → Maasai Mara

    An 8am city escape in a 4×4 land-cruiser, descending the Rift Valley escarpment with a viewpoint stop and a picnic lunch en route. You reach camp by mid-afternoon, settle into canvas, then head straight out for an introduction game drive — ending with a sundowner as the plains go gold.

  2. 2

    Day 2

    The Mara, in full

    Dawn departure while the cats are still working — a full morning with prides, cheetah coalitions and the river crossings in season, plus a bush breakfast where it happens. After a siesta at camp, the afternoon drive tracks the day's rumours until sunset, and the evening ends around the campfire with the night sky doing its loudest thing.

  3. 3

    Day 3

    Final encounters → Nairobi

    One last dawn drive — the Mara repays early risers — then brunch and an easy exit with a curio stop at a Maasai women's cooperative. You're back in Nairobi by early evening, filled with stories and about 400 new favourite photos.

Amboseli & the Great Mountain

Nairobi → Amboseli → Nairobi

  • Big tusker game drives
  • Maasai cultural village visit
  • All park fees & full board

Not included

  • International flights
  • Kenya eTA & visa fees
  • Travel insurance (we can arrange it)
  • Tips, drinks & souvenirs
  • Night-drive park surcharges

The journey, day by day

  1. 1

    Day 1

    Nairobi → Amboseli

    South through Kajiado rangelands to the Namanga border town and onto Amboseli's dusty amphitheatre. Check in, watch the mountain do its cloud choreography, and take a first game drive among the swamp-edge herds before dark.

  2. 2

    Day 2

    Tuskers & swamps

    A full day shaped around the light: big tusker families at Longjango and Enkongo swamps in the morning, Observation Hill's panorama at noon, and an evening drive composed entirely of silhouettes — elephants, dust, and Kilimanjaro going pink.

  3. 3

    Day 3

    Culture & the mountain's edge

    Morning drive, then a visit to a Maasai manyatta arranged through our community partners — songs, stories and a proper demonstration of how beadwork funds schooling. Sunset from the lodge deck; night drive option in conservancy land.

  4. 4

    Day 4

    Dawn portraits → Nairobi

    A final short drive to catch the first light on the big tuskers one last time, breakfast, and the road back north. Arrive Nairobi by late afternoon.

Bush & Beach Odyssey

Maasai Mara → Nairobi → Diani Beach

Couples & families7 Days / 6 Nights

From, per person$1,890

What's included

  • 3 nights Mara safari, full board
  • 3 nights Diani beachfront resort
  • Internal flights & transfers
  • Sundowner dhow cruise

Not included

  • International flights
  • Kenya eTA & visa fees
  • Travel insurance (we can arrange it)
  • Tips, drinks & souvenirs
  • Optional spa treatments & water sports gear

The journey, day by day

  1. 1

    Days 1–3

    Maasai Mara — the wild act

    A light-aircraft hop from Nairobi Wilson drops you onto a savanna airstrip where your guide is already waiting. Three nights of full-board camp life: dawn and dusk drives, a picnic breakfast where the lions had breakfast, optional balloon dawn, and evenings that smell of woodsmoke and sound like nothing else.

  2. 2

    Day 4

    Plains → powder

    Fly the Mara strip straight to Ukunda on the south coast — the bush-to-beach transfer that makes this itinerary famous. By afternoon you're barefoot on Diani, and by evening there's seafood with your toes still in the sand.

  3. 3

    Days 5–7

    Diani — the exhale act

    Three nights of reef-protected calm: snorkelling over the coral gardens, a Wasini Island dhow day with a Swahili lunch, a sunset cruise with dolphins if the sea is showing off, and a final lazy breakfast before the short flight back to Nairobi.

04 · 1 Day (sunrise)

Balloon Dawn over the Mara

Mara Conservancy launch site

The unforgettable add-on1 Day (sunrise)

From, per person$450

What's included

  • Pre-dawn balloon flight over the plains
  • Champagne bush breakfast
  • Flight certificate & photos
  • Game drive en route back

Not included

The journey, day by day

  1. 1

    The Day

    The sky at first light

    A pre-dawn 4×4 transfer to the launch site while the plains are still monochrome. You stand in the basket as the burners breathe fire, and then the world's oldest form of flight does what it has always done: it goes wherever the wind is going. An hour drifting at giraffe-height — herds parting beneath you, the river stitching silver through the grass — ending with a champagne bush breakfast and your pilot-signed flight certificate, before a slow game drive back to camp.
